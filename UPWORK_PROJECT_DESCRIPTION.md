- CSV exports for Excel or Power BI
- A client-ready HTML dashboard

## Best For

- Social media managers
- Small businesses
- Marketing agencies
- Creators and personal brands
- Campaign reporting

Advanced: $140 - custom metrics, campaign comparison, and written insights

2 to 5 days depending on dataset size and reporting scope.
